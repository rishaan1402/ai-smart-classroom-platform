## What this means for the design

1. **Room-mic CCTV audio can't be transcribed.** Both CCTV clips with sound give Whisper (large-v3) zero detected speech. This confirms the plan: the **teacher lapel mic is required for ASR**, and the room mic is only for noise and chatter features. None of the footage has usable teacher speech, so the ASR and topic pipeline needs different test audio: a recorded lecture, or a lapel recording from the pilot school.
2. **Detection misses students in dense scenes.** YOLO11n-pose at 1280 px finds 15–17 of ~45 students in the desk-level footage and misses back-corner kids in Securus. Next: a bigger model (yolo11m/l-pose) plus tiled inference (SAHI-style) on the back half of the frame.
3. **Camera angle decides what's measurable.**
   - High corner (Securus): best overall. Full bodies, head direction readable, the teacher is visible.
   - Top-down (Altima): good for exam mode (hands, papers, neighbour interaction), but mostly the tops of heads, so **faces aren't recognisable** and gaze is unreliable.
   - Desk-level (Lolo): heavy occlusion; only front rows are analysable. Not a realistic install position.
   - For the pilot, recommend front-wall cameras above the board at ~2.5 m with a 15–30° downward tilt, 4MP.
4. **The face-keypoint visibility metric overstates** head-down and top-down cases (the pose model hallucinates nose and eye points). Head pose needs a dedicated face-crop model (6DRepNet), not COCO keypoints.
5. **Masks and uniforms** (Lolo): identity must lean on the seat prior; face matching alone won't hold.
6. **Rights**: the Lolo videos carry a Bilibili watermark (教物理的小龙老师), so the YouTube channel is likely a re-upload. Use is internal R&D only.
